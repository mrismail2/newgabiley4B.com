Platform Features

Everything a School Needs,
All in One Place

From student registration to payment tracking, attendance to exam results — School Plus covers the full lifecycle of school management.

Complete School Isolation

Each school operates in its own secure environment. No school can ever access another school’s students, payments, or records. Enforced at the database level with Supabase RLS.

Role-Based Access Control

Five distinct roles: Super Admin, School Admin, Teacher, Accountant, and Parent. Each role sees only what they need — nothing more.

Payment Management

Track monthly fees, record payments, generate unpaid student lists, and view monthly revenue reports. Supports paid, unpaid, partial, and free status.

Attendance Tracking

Mark daily attendance by class. Track present, absent, and late. Generate attendance reports per student, class, or date range.

Exams & Results

Create exams, enter student marks, calculate grades, and generate academic reports. Parents can view their child’s results directly.

Reports & Analytics

Export reports to PDF and Excel. School Admins get school-level reports. The Super Admin gets platform-wide analytics across all schools.
